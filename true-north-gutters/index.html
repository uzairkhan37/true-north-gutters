Good questions

Frequently asked.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Most homes need it twice a year — once in late spring after pollen and seed-pods drop, and once in late fall after the leaves are down. Homes with overhanging trees often need a third cleaning.

Do I need to be home for the appointment?

Nope. As long as we have access to the exterior of the home and a power source if needed, we'll get the job done and send you photos when we're finished.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the home, the number of stories, and how clogged things are. Most single-story cleanings fall in a predictable range — call or email for a free quote in under 5 minutes.

What if you find a problem while you're up there?

We'll take photos, show you what we see, and give you a no-pressure recommendation. Small fixes (resealing, refastening) we can usually do on the spot. Bigger issues we'll quote separately.

Are you insured?

Yes, we carry liability insurance on every job. We're happy to provide our certificate of insurance on request.

What's included with cleaning?

Hand-clearing every section, flushing the downspouts to verify water flow, bagging and hauling away all debris, a quick visual inspection for damage, and before/after photos.
